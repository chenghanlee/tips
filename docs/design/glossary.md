# Tips

Tips helps people discover practical AI techniques and assess firsthand reports of using them. These terms follow the agreed product and technical designs.

## Language

**Tip**:
A practical AI technique described by someone who has used it firsthand, including when to use it, the steps and what happened.
_Avoid_: Article, verified solution.

**Review**:
A firsthand account of trying a particular tip and what happened. It is not a clarification question, general comment or reply.
_Avoid_: Comment, testimonial, discussion reply.

**Helpfulness vote**:
A reader's Helpful or Not helpful assessment of a tip or review. It does not establish that the reader tried the technique or that it succeeded.
_Avoid_: Success rate, verified result.

**Reported setup**:
The primary model and environment reported for the author's own attempt. A tip's setup and a review's setup describe separate attempts.
_Avoid_: Compatibility guarantee, supported platform.

**Supporting evidence**:
Optional prompt text, result text or media accompanying a firsthand account. Its presence does not establish that the account is truthful.
_Avoid_: Verification, proof of effectiveness.

**Report**:
A reader's submission about a suspected community-rule violation on a tip or review. Failure of a technique or disagreement alone is not a violation.
_Avoid_: Negative review, not-helpful vote.

**Moderator-hidden content**:
A tip or review deliberately removed from ordinary public visibility by a moderator. Its author and moderators retain the access allowed by the content rules.
_Avoid_: Author-deleted content, shadow ban.

**Author-deleted content**:
A tip or review the author has irreversibly removed. Deleting a tip does not delete independently visible reviews of it.
_Avoid_: Moderator-hidden content, reversible archive.

**Full ban**:
An account restriction that prevents contributions while retaining sign-in, reading, deletion of the account's own content and account deletion. Lifting it does not restore removed content or invalidated votes.
_Avoid_: Account deletion, shadow ban.
